A LONG-RUNNING CONVERSATION

Topology, together.

The seminar began in Chicago in the mid-1960s as a way for topologists to exchange recent developments. It has grown into a regular gathering of algebraic and geometric topologists from across the region, with speakers from around the world.

Meetings usually take place in fall, winter and spring. Students are especially welcome: the seminar is a place to make contacts and become part of the wider mathematical community.

Regular participants come from Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ontario, Ohio, Kentucky, Wisconsin, Minnesota and Kansas.
