Midwest Topology Seminar

Northwestern University

March 10, 2012

Speakers

10.00AMEmily Riehl Harvard University Lifting properties and the small object argument
11.30AMJohn Francis Northwestern University Factorization homology and low-dimensional topology
2.30PMMark Behrens MIT Exotic Spheres via Topological Modular Forms
4.00PMMike Hopkins Harvard University Equivariant multiplicative closure

All talks are in Swift Hall 107, with coffee and a light breakfast before the first talk. Immediately following the last talk, there will be a reception in Harris Hall 108. The math department is in Lunt Hall and there is parking in the lot just north of Lunt.

Note: Swift Hall is immediately east of Lunt Hall. Don't confuse Swift Hall with Annie May Swift Hall, which is further away.
