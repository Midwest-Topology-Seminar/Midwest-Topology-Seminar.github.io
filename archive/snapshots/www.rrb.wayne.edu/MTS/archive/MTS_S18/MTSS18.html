Announcement

The Spring 2018 Midwest Topology Seminar will be held at Indiana University in Bloomington, Indiana on April 28, 2018. We expect the first talk to start at 10:00am EDT (preceded by coffee) and the last talk to end at 5:00pm EDT (followed by refreshments). We ask those planning to attend to register on our registration page so that we have a good idea of how many people to expect.

Confirmed Speakers

  • Julie Bergner (Virginia)
  • Jonathan Campbell (Vanderbilt)
  • Guillem Cazassus (IU)
  • Charles Weibel (Rutgers)

Breaks will be in the Rawles Hall lounge (map) and talks will be in Swain East 105 (map).

Please see the schedule page for more details (when available).

Financial Support

Travel support provided from an NSF grant through Northwestern University has now all been allocated.

Local Information

We have some discounted room blocks at local hotels. Please see the travel page for details.

Time Zone

Bloomington, Indiana, is in the Eastern time zone and observes daylight saving time. See timeanddate.com to compare with your local time.
