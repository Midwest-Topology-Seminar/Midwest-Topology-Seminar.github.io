Notre Dame/South bend dining options

$ = more expensive, $$ = downright fancy. Lunch options are on campus or at Eddy st commons. Some near campus options decent for dinner, other dinner options likely will take you off campus. The restaurant lists for downtown south bend and beyond are somewhat curated.

Downtown South Bend (1 mile from Residence Inn)

  • Fiddler's Hearth - 127 N Main St (Irish pub)
  • LaSalle Grill ($$) - 115 W Colfax Ave - 1st floor (fine dining)
  • Gastropub at LaSalle Grill - 115 W Colfax Ave - 3rd floor (affordable alternative to LaSalle Grill, good selection of local beers)
  • The India Garden - 123 N Michigan St (Indian)
  • Woochi Japanese Fusion ($) - 123 W Washington St (Decent Sushi and Japanese fusion)
  • Cafe Navarre ($$) - 101 N Michigan (fine dining in historic bank building)
  • South Bend Brew Werks - 216 S Michigan St (small microbrewery)
  • Cambodian Thai - 235 S Michigan St

Surrounding area - South Bend/Mishawaka/Granger (5-15 min drive)

  • Evil Czech - 3703 N Main St, Mishawaka (brewery with locally sourced menu)
  • Crooked Ewe - 1047 Lincoln Way E, South Bend (another brewery with locally sourced menu - not good for large parties - crowded)
  • Javier's Bistro - 2007 Miami St, South Bend (Puerto Rican inspired)
  • JW Chen's - 1835 South Bend Ave, South Bend ("Home style" Chinese)
  • Seoul Garden - 1025 W McKinley Ave, Mishawaka (Korean)
  • Elia's Mediterranean Cuisine - 2128 South Bend Ave, South Bend
  • Soho Japanese Bistro ($) - 7225 Heritage Square Dr - Heritage Square Shopping Center (Decent Sushi and Japanese grill)
  • Capri Italian Restaurant ($) - 50827 Princess Way, Granger
