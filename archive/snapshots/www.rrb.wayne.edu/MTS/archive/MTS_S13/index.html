Continuing the now decades-long tradition, the Spring 2013 Midwest Topology Seminar will be held May 11 and 12, 2013 at the University of Kentucky.

 

Speakers
Mohammed Abouzaid - Columbia University Michael Ching - Amherst College
John Lind - Johns Hopkins University Charles Rezk - University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign
Kirsten Wickelgren - Harvard University Bruce Williams - University of Notre Dame

 

If you are planning to attend, please register soon.

 

Some funds will be available to support travel costs of participants, especially for graduate students and junior faculty without other support.  Please note that the deadline for funding requests was April 19.

 

There will be a hiking excursion to Raven Run Nature Sanctuary on Sunday afternoon. For more information, see the Attractions page.

 

 

Funding for this meeting comes from the University of Kentucky College of Arts and Sciences, Vice President for Research, and Department of Mathematics.
