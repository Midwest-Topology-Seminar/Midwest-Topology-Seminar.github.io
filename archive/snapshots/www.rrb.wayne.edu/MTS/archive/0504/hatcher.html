Wicket Subgroups of Braid Groups (Allen Hatcher)

Abstract:

This talk represents joint work with Tara Brendle. We show the space of configurations of n disjoint semicircles (wickets) in upper half-space meeting the boundary plane orthogonally in their endpoints is aspherical. Its fundamental group W_n is a subgroup of the braid group on 2n strands, and contains two copies of the braid group on n strands. There is a nice presentation of W_n in terms of these two subgroups together with simple twists of the wickets. The group W_n is closely related to the fundamental group of the space of configurations of n disjoint circles in 3-space, the so-called symmetric automorphism group of a free group.
