Midwest Topology Seminar

Northwestern University

April 24, 2010

Schedule

9.30 Coffee Swift 107
10.00 Jack Morava (Johns Hopkins) Base motives and homotopic descent (Abstract)
11:30 Dan Isaksen (Wayne State) Motivic stable homotopy groups (Abstract)
2:30 Angelica Osorno (MIT) An infinite loop space structure for K-theory of bimonoidal categories
4:00 Soren Galatius (Stanford) Monoids of moduli spaces of manifolds
5:15 approx Reception Common Room, Lunt Hall

All talks will be held in Swift Hall 107. Swift Hall is immediately behind Lunt Hall, at 2033 Sheridan Rd, which is the home of the Mathematics Department. Parking is available in the Garrett lot, next to the Math Department and in other adjacent lots. Maps of Northwestern University
