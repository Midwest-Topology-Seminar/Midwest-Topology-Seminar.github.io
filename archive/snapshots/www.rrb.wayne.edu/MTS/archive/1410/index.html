Speakers

    10.00AMEmanuele Dotto MIT Trace methods in Real algebraic K-theory
    11.30AMGreg Arone University of Virginia Bredon homology of partition posets, a theorem of Kuhn, and its ku analogue

All talks are in Swift Hall 107, with coffee and a light breakfast before the first talk. Immediately following the last talk, there will be a reception in Harris Hall 108. The math department is in Lunt Hall and there is parking in the lot just north of Lunt.

    Note: Swift Hall is immediately east of Lunt Hall. Don't confuse Swift Hall with Annie May Swift Hall, which is further away.

Travel and Support

    The Midwest Topology Seminar is supported by the National Science Foundation and Northwestern University.

    A certain amount of travel support is available, especially for graduate students and mathematicians with postdoctoral appointments. If you'd like support please please indicate this when you register below.

    (October 1) Funding is now very limited, but we will do what we can. Please write directly to an organizer.

Registration and Lodging

    The Midwest Topology Seminar is free and open to the public, but your registration will help with planning the coffees and reception. You may also indicate if you're requesting support or if you'd like help finding a hotel. The registration page is
    Registration MTS October 2014
    A list of hotels and other visitor information is available at Math Department Visitor Info

    If you make a reservation at an Evanston Hotel mention that you are attending a mathematics conference at Northwestern University. The University has a mildly discounted rate at most local hotels.
