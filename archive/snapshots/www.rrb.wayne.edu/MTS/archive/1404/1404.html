The Spring 2014 meeting of the Midwest Topology Seminar will be held at IUPUI in Indianapolis, Indiana on Saturday April 19.

Registration

Please help us plan the meeting by registering in advance. There is no registration fee.
Registration form

Confirmed Speakers

  • Andrew Blumberg, UT Austin: The nilpotence theorem for the K-theory of the sphere spectrum
  • Jim Fowler, Ohio State University, Columbus: Aspherical manifolds that cannot be triangulated
  • Lars Hesselholt, Nagoya University, Japan: The K-theory assembly map
  • Niles Johnson, Ohio State University, Newark: Modeling stable two-types
  • Ayelet Lindenstrauss, Indiana University, Bloomington: Higher Hochschild and Topological Hochschild Homology
  • Ben Williams, University of British Columbia, Vancouver: A Simplicial EHP Spectral Sequence in A1 Homotopy Theory

All Saturday lectures will be in INIT 252 (IT building, across the parking lot from the Math Department building).

Schedule and Abstracts

First talk starts at 9:30AM Eastern time; last talk ends at 6:00PM Eastern time.

Full schedule and abstracts are available here.

  • Lars Hesselholt, The Big DeRham-Witt Complex

Financial Support

Some funds are available to partially support travel costs of participants, especially for graduate students and junior faculty without other support. (Funding is very limited and priority will go to those working in the Midwest, broadly construed.) The deadline for applying for funding has expired and all funds have been allocated.

You may request funding through the registration form in case more funding becomes available.
