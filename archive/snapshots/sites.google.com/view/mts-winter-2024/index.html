When and where

The 2024 Winter edition of the Midwest Topology Seminar will take place at Loyola University Chicago 

(Lake Shore Campus) on March 16-17, 2024.

All talks will be at the Life Science Building LSB 142. (1050 W. Sheridan Rd.)

Registration and coffee breaks will be at LSB 142

Speakers

  • Julie Bergner  (University of Virginia)

  • Colleen Delaney (UC Berkeley)

  • Paul Kirk  (Indiana University Bloomington)

  • Cary Malkiewich (Binghamton University)

  • Chris Schommer-Pries (University of Notre Dame)

  • Inna Zakharevich (Cornell University)

There will also be a session of contributed talks. If you would like to contribute a short talk, please register and indicate your title and abstract in the registration form by February 28.

Registration  

If you plan to attend this Midwest Topology Seminar, please complete the registration form. 

Contributed talks: 

The registration form allows you to suggest a contributed talk (by February 28).

Funding:

The Midwest Topology Seminar is funded by a grant from the National Science Foundation, which provides limited support for students and junior faculty to offset the cost of attendance. Everyone in need of support should apply by filling out the registration form and indicating their interest in financial support by February 28, 2024 (we appreciate early applications). Funding decisions will be made shortly thereafter.

Conference dinner:

There will be a conference dinner on Saturday at 6:30 pm. This will be at Beard and Belly (6157 N Broadway, Chicago, IL 60660)
