Travel to East Lansing:

By Car:  East Lansing is within driving range of many places in the Midwest.

By Plane: Nearby airports including Lansing (LAN) and Detroit (DTW). The Lansing airport is a short cab or Uber ride from campus. If you fly into Detroit you can rent a car to drive to East Lansing, or there is a bus shuttle to East Lansing, called the Michigan Flyer. 

By Train: There is Amtrak service to East Lansing. The Amtrak station is on the southwest corner of campus.
