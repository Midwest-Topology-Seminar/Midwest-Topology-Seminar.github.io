The Fall 2017 meeting of the Midwest Topology Seminar will be held at Wayne
State University in Detroit, Michigan on Saturday 11 November.

Four speakers will give 1-hour talks:

Gabe Angelini-Knoll, Michigan State University
Cary Malkiewich, Binghamton University
Stephan Stolz, University of Notre Dame
Liz Vidaurre, University of Rochester

Please visit the seminar's website
www.clas.wayne.edu/disaksen/mts2017
to register.  This will help us plan a successful meeting.

We have reserved blocks of rooms at two hotels.  One block expires on
October 6, and the other block expires on October 20.  You may wish to make
preliminary travel plans before those dates.

Some funding is available for participant travel support.  Priority will be
given to graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and others with
limited sources of travel support.  Women and members of historically
underrepresented groups are especially encouraged to apply.  You can apply
for funding through the registration page.

Contact Dan Isaksen at isaksen at wayne.edu for questions.

Thanks,

Robert Bruner, Dan Isaksen, and Andrew Salch
